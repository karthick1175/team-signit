How a cabin gets built

  1. Step 1Plan the layoutReception, rooms, pantry and washrooms drawn to fit your site.
  2. Step 2Build the steel frameMS frame, cladding and roof welded in our Bengaluru workshop.
  3. Step 3Finish the insideWalls, ceiling, glass, flooring, lights and fittings done before it leaves.
  4. Step 4Deliver and set upLifted by crane onto your site, ready to work in within 60–75 days.

Structure

  • Heavy-duty MS bottom and top framing
  • Side posts and wall stiffeners
  • Steel roofing and outer cladding
  • Lifting points for crane shifting

Interiors

  • Plywood and gypsum walls, putty and paint
  • Matching false ceiling
  • Insulated walls and roof
  • Carpet flooring, clad wet areas

Glass and windows

  • Saint-Gobain toughened double glass entrance
  • Glass doors for meeting rooms
  • Fixed toughened glass panels
  • UPVC sliding windows with mesh

Washrooms

  • Separate gents' and ladies' blocks
  • Jaguar or Kohler fittings
  • Granite counter basins with mirror
  • Exhaust fans throughout

Electricals

  • Concealed copper wiring with MCB
  • Philips, Wipro or Surya LED lights
  • LAN and HDMI points
  • Proper earthing and roof ventilation
